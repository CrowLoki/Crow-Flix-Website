# CrowFlix Free Collection

The CrowFlix Free Collection is a branded discovery collection of direct links
to official publisher pages that offer free material. It is not a video host,
an IPTV source, a player proxy, a scraper, or a mirror.

## Collection rules

- Each destination opens the publisher's original page only after the visitor
  presses **Open website**.
- CrowFlix does not fetch, embed, cache, download, relay, copy, or redistribute
  a destination's video catalogue.
- A destination must be an official publisher, broadcaster, studio,
  competition, archive, or verified rights-holder channel page. Promotional
  pages are allowed only when they are plainly labelled as such; nothing is
  represented as a full movie, series, or live-TV service unless the publisher
  itself offers it there.
- Free availability, region restrictions, advertising, age gates, account
  requirements, and catalogue changes remain controlled by the publisher and
  its platform.
- CrowFlix may remove a destination when the publisher, platform, availability,
  or rights indication changes.

## Curation provenance

The initial selection was researched from the public
[Free Official YouTube Content](https://github.com/SuperAB123/Free-Official-Youtube-Content)
directory and then stored as direct publisher-page links. The directory is a
research lead, not a CrowFlix content provider and is not displayed as the
collection's public brand.

CrowFlix remains the curator and interface. The individual publishers retain
ownership of their names, logos, video, and platform pages.
